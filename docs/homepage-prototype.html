Montpelier City Fire Department

Montpelier, Vermont

Three arched bays in downtown brick, cut into a hillside block of the smallest state capital in the country. Montpelier runs one of Vermont's few mostly-career departments — 17 career firefighters backed by 12 paid-per-call members — covering the State House, the Winooski River floodplain, and mutual aid across Washington County.

Vermont is unusual this way: of 231 departments in the database, only seven are fully career. The rest are staffed by neighbors.

About the project

Preserving Vermont's firehouse record.

FD Vermont is an independent archive of the state's fire service: one page for every department, built from public E911 and VCGI address data and filled in with original photography, apparatus rosters, and station history.

Vermont's fire service is overwhelmingly volunteer — 3,261 volunteers and 1,394 paid-per-call members against 531 career firefighters. Small departments change quietly: a station is replaced, a company merges, a hand-painted sign comes down. This is an attempt to photograph them before that happens.

Department records are current as of the last database update in April 2026. Counts come from department self-reporting and are incomplete in places — corrections are welcome and credited.
